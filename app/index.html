The pain point

India's tradespeople work with their hands. Billing apps need both.

Most independent electricians, plumbers, AC and appliance technicians, carpenters and painters work alone, with no office staff. They use Android phones and UPI every day, but billing apps such as Tally, Zoho or Vyapar assume a keyboard and English. So they quote a lump sum out loud, or scribble it on a scrap of paper.

Why existing tools don't work

  • Typing-heavy screens. Dropdowns and one-item-at-a-time forms don't suit busy hands and a rushed schedule.
  • Language mismatch. Workers mix languages mid-sentence: “dedh hazaar”, “do inch CPVC elbow”. Typing that is slow and error-prone.
  • Accounting jargon. “SKU”, “subtotal” and “ledger” mean nothing on the job site.
  • A keyboard mic isn't enough. It only transcribes. What's missing is software that understands a whole spoken job summary.

The problem in one line

Solo Indian tradespeople lose money, time and credibility because creating an itemized bill and collecting payment on the job site means typing in English-first apps they can't use with busy hands.

Voice in. A bill the customer can trust out.

How to use KaamBill

From “job done” to “payment received” in five steps.

A step bar inside the app always shows where you are. First time? Set your name, UPI ID and rate card once. After that, every bill starts with your voice.

  1. 1

    Saaman Items

    Tap the mic and describe the job, or tap “Likh ke daalein” to type or dictate with Wispr Flow.

  2. 2

    Jaanch Check

    Answer one question at a time (a missing price, an unsure quantity), then confirm the total with “haan”.

  3. 3

    Details

    Check the amount and your UPI ID, add the customer's WhatsApp number, and pick UPI, Cash or Baad mein (later).

  4. 4

    Payment

    The customer scans the QR with the amount filled in. You tap “Paisa mil gaya” (or “Cash mila”).

  5. 5

    Bill

    The bill is marked PAID or DUE. WhatsApp opens with the PDF and you press Send.

Say it like you'd tell a friend

Questions

Frequently asked

Which languages does KaamBill understand?

Hinglish, Hindi and English, including mixing them in one sentence. The app language you pick changes the screen text, the mic language and the app's messages together.

Is this a GST invoice?

No. KaamBill produces a simple Bill or Estimate, and labels it that way. GST invoice mode is on the roadmap.

Can KaamBill see whether the customer has paid?

Not yet. “Paid” is the worker's own confirmation after they see the payment. Automatic payment status from UPI is on the roadmap.

What if I say the wrong amount, or it mishears?

Nothing reaches the customer until you've seen the total on screen and approved it. You can edit any item, quantity or price before then.

Where is my customers' data stored?

Customer names and phone numbers are stored only on your device. The text of what you say is sent to an AI service to turn it into bill items.

Does it work without a good network?

The app loads offline, and you can finish and share a bill that's already been built. Understanding a new sentence needs a connection; if you're offline it's queued so you can retry.
